## Interpretation Notes

- `canonical_entity`, `hard_rule`, and `conditional_rule` are the main Phase 4 implementation targets.
- `guidance`, `example`, `operational_fact`, and `future_rental_fact` are important, but they must not be flattened into deterministic rule rows without further governance.
