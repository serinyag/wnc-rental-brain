## Phase 4 Exclusions From Authoritative Structuring

- historical-case pricing and operational patterns
- email wording
- proposal example line items
- guidance such as collaboration pricing strategy or sensory fit of food
- unresolved waiver criteria
- live operational counts that the sources explicitly say must be confirmed case by case
