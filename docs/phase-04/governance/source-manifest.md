## Source Handling Notes

- The Knowledge Inventory explicitly states that editable masters control their exported PDFs and that PDFs should not be maintained independently.
- Historical cases are explicitly reference-only and excluded from authoritative rule retrieval.
- `WNC Rental Informal Rules.xlsm` is useful for identifying unresolved policy areas, not for direct rule automation.
- The pricing workbook pair and the editable-master-versus-export pairs are the main conflict areas that require human review before broader Phase 4 rule loading beyond the currently implemented booking-fee, payment, expedited-surcharge, cancellation, and capacity slices.
