# Phase 6.2D Historical Precedent Statement & Contamination-Control Foundation

Date: August 7, 2026

## Summary

Phase 6.2D implements the governed semantic statement layer for Phase 6 historical precedent.

It adds:

- version-scoped historical responsibility statements
- version-scoped historical decision statements
- version-scoped historical lesson statements
- explicit analyst-inference typing
- statement-level historical-value and contamination metadata
- statement-level provenance links back to Phase 6.2B evidence associations
- activation-time provenance enforcement
- case-version historical-value summary synchronization
- draft-only editability for statements and statement provenance
- private-by-default RLS and grant posture

This task intentionally stops before Phase 4 or Phase 5 relationship tables, production ingestion, retrieval, chunking, embeddings, or answer generation.

## Responsibility Semantics

`public.historical_case_version_responsibilities` records actor-specific historical responsibility inside one governed case version.

Approved actor types:

- `wnc`
- `client`
- `external_supplier`

Meaning:

- `wnc` captures responsibility historically carried by WNC in that case
- `client` captures responsibility historically carried by the client in that case
- `external_supplier` captures responsibility historically carried by an outside vendor or supplier in that case

This table records what responsibility existed in one historical precedent. It does not declare what WNC, a client, or a supplier must do today.

## Historical Decision Semantics

`public.historical_case_version_decisions` records material decisions made within the historical case version.

Examples of what belongs here:

- a storage method used in that case
- a supplier arrangement accepted in that case
- a production choice made in that case
- a brand-boundary decision made in that case

These are historical event facts or governed historical reconstructions. They are not Phase 4 rules, Phase 5 current knowledge, or current pricing and operating policy.

## Lesson Semantics

`public.historical_case_version_lessons` distinguishes four required lesson kinds:

- `source_explicit`
  - the source itself explicitly states the lesson
- `curated_lesson`
  - a human curator wrote a reusable lesson supported by the case
- `analyst_inference`
  - an interpretation derived from the supported facts rather than explicitly stated by the source
- `caution_warning`
  - a warning preserved mainly to prevent contamination or repetition of a known issue

`analyst_inference` stays structurally distinct through `lesson_kind` and still requires evidence linkage before activation. That preserves `P6-INV-006 — Derived Artifact Boundary`.

## Historical-Value Protection

All three statement families carry:

- `historical_value_only`
- `contamination_risk_level`
- `current_authority_disposition`

`historical_value_only` means the statement is worth preserving historically but must not be treated as current operational truth.

`contamination_risk_level` is controlled to:

- `low`
- `medium`
- `high`

`current_authority_disposition` is controlled to:

- `no_current_rule_implication`
- `check_phase_4`
- `check_phase_5`
- `check_phase_4_and_5`
- `potential_conflict_with_current_knowledge`
- `current_status_unknown`

Implemented validation choices:

- every governed statement must carry explicit risk and authority-disposition metadata
- `high` contamination risk cannot be paired with `no_current_rule_implication`
- the same validation function is reused across responsibilities, decisions, and lessons

### Case-version summary behavior

Phase 6.2A introduced `historical_case_versions.contains_historical_value_only_content`.

In 6.2D that summary becomes statement-grounded:

- after responsibility, decision, or lesson inserts/updates/deletes, triggers recompute the parent summary
- parent updates also recompute the summary before lifecycle validation

Result:

- if any governed statement is `historical_value_only = true`, the case-version summary becomes `true`
- if no governed statement is `historical_value_only = true`, the summary becomes `false`

This prevents obvious drift between the parent summary and child statement reality.

## Statement Provenance

The provenance chain is:

statement
→ `historical_case_version_*_sources`
→ `historical_case_version_source_objects`
→ `knowledge_source_objects`

Statements do not link directly to `knowledge_source_objects`.

Reason:

- `historical_case_version_source_objects` already stores the governed case-version evidence relationship
- that relationship already carries:
  - evidence role
  - source locator
  - evidence strength
  - supported claim dimensions
  - confidentiality

The statement-link layer therefore reuses the governed evidence association instead of bypassing it.

### Provenance integrity

6.2D enforces:

- statement and evidence link rows must belong to the same `historical_case_version_id`
- duplicate statement-to-evidence links are rejected
- one evidence association may support many statements
- one statement may carry many evidence associations
- statement links must use a source association whose `supported_claim_dimensions` includes the matching family:
  - `responsibility`
  - `decision`
  - `lesson`

### Activation-time traceability

Draft statements may temporarily exist without provenance.

Before a case version becomes `active`, `private.assert_historical_case_version_statement_provenance_complete(bigint)` blocks activation if any responsibility, decision, or lesson lacks at least one valid statement-level evidence link.

That preserves `P6-INV-005 — Provenance` without blocking draft authoring workflow.

## Immutability

Statements and their provenance links are part of the governed case-version snapshot.

Implemented behavior:

- draft versions may insert, update, and delete statements
- draft versions may insert, update, and delete statement-evidence links
- once the parent case version is no longer `draft`, all three statement tables and all three statement-link tables become immutable
- activation still runs through the Phase 6.2A lifecycle trigger, now extended with summary synchronization and provenance-completeness validation

This preserves `P6-INV-009 — Version Supersession`.
